Why it exists

It comes from a real operation. Animals fed on schedule, plants on rotation, the ledger written before coffee. The first thing this operation taught me: the plants had been skipped, because the reminder lived in someone else's check-in instead of on the ground.

A homestead ledger is cost control, not income. You eat the eggs. Surplus goes to the animals.

Then the second lesson: a county bill of 560 dollars became 37 hours of neighbor work, paid in hours instead of cash, logged line by line. The ground holds when the money is honest.

The record-keeping should not eat your mornings. That is what this pack is for.
